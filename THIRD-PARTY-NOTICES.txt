TextBlob's bundled English spelling-frequency file states that it is based on
public-domain Project Gutenberg books and frequency lists from Wiktionary and
the British National Corpus (including Peter Norvig's big.txt aggregation).

TextBlob's bundled English POS lexicon states that it was derived from Eric
Brill's rule-based tagger resources (MIT/University of Pennsylvania; MIT
license) with additional words from the Twitter Part-of-Speech Annotated Data
(TweetNLP), available under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.

TweetNLP attribution cited by that resource:
Kevin Gimpel, Nathan Schneider, Brendan O'Connor, et al. (2011).
"Part-of-Speech Tagging for Twitter: Annotation, Features, and Experiments."
Proceedings of the Annual Meeting of the ACL, Portland, Oregon.

The final Vocabulary Tracker curriculum was further filtered, de-inflected,
leveled, tagged, and supplemented with app-authored practical vocabulary lists.
