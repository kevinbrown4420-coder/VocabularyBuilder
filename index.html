                  App settings

                  Appearance

                  Study sounds

                  Off by default. Short, quiet tones for correct and incorrect answers; they do not replace TalkBack announcements or pronunciation recordings.

                  Optional online dictionaries

                  Built-in study is free and works without registration or API keys. You may add your own Merriam-Webster keys for extra lookups, pronunciation recordings, and synonyms. Those additional services require internet access. Keys stay on this device.

                  Merriam-Webster API Developer Center

                  Offline use

                  Checking the offline study pack…

                  After opening the app online once and letting it install its files, the built-in lessons and your progress work without internet. Device-generated pronunciation requires an installed offline voice; optional dictionary recordings still require internet.

                  Backup and reset

                  Backups contain study progress and settings, but never your API keys.

                  About this app

                  Vocabulary Builder

                  Version 1.8

                  Accessibility-first vocabulary practice with 1,155 built-in definitions and examples available for offline study. Optional online reference information is available with your own keys.

                  Navigation

                  Android Back returns to the previous app screen and is trapped on Home so it does not intentionally exit the installed app. During a round, Back returns Home and keeps that round available to resume.

                  Data and reference content

                  Study progress stays on this device and does not require an account. The 1,155 built-in teaching definitions and examples are original educational content. Optional dictionary keys also stay on this device. Online Merriam-Webster reference information and recordings are separate from offline practice.
